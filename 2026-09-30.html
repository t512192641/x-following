Opus 5.5 发布后，满屏都是“一句话生成”的动画视频。

但 Opus 5.5 并不是视频模型，它连视频都看不了。这些视频全是它写的程序画出来的。

原理很简单：视频就是一串静态图，每秒 30 张。做视频只需要回答一个问题：第 N 帧长什么样？Opus 写的就是能回答这个问题的程序，给它任意一个时间点，画面就唯一确定。

整条流水线：脚本分镜 → 用 HTML/SVG/three.js 画画面 → 用 GSAP 把动作钉在时间轴上 → TTS 配音并拿到每个字的时间戳 → 无头 Chrome 逐帧截图 → FFmpeg 合成 MP4。

它和 Seedance、可灵是两条路：它们直接生成像素，Opus 生成的是代码。

所以代码视频不会出错字，改一处细节只要改一行代码，和旁白能对齐到帧；但人脸、动物、写实画面，视频模型强得多。两条路也能混着用：让 Claude 当导演，通过 MCP 去调用视频模型。

代价是：很烧额度（一支 3 分钟带旁白的短片大约用掉周额度的 7%），审美还是要人把关。

想自己试的话，HeyGen 开源的 HyperFrames 和 Remotion 都有现成的 Claude Code 技能包，装上就能用。

一句话：Opus 5.5 能做视频，靠的是长时间规划加写代码，而不是视频能力。

I made a prompt that solves any problem with what you already have, using the way NASA saved Apollo 13.

In April 1970 an oxygen tank blew up 200,000 miles from Earth. The crew was running out of clean air, and the spare filters were square while the socket was round. Engineers in Houston tipped out a copy of everything they had on board then they built a fix from that pile.

It works just as well on a budget that won't stretch, a project with no time left, or a business you're starting with nothing.

Paste this into your AI and answer honestly.

---

You are a NASA flight engineer, and we are working my problem the way Houston worked Apollo 13.

My problem: [what's wrong, and what needs to happen]

Ask me one question at a time and wait for each answer: everything I have right now, money, time, tools, people and skills, even the things that seem useless. Then what I can't get, no matter what. Then what "good enough to get home" looks like.

Then give me: 1. Everything on the table, as a list, including what I dismissed 2. Three ways to solve it using only that list 3. The one I can build today, step by step 4. What it won't fix, so I know what waits until I'm home

Rules: nothing goes in the solution that isn't on the table, no step starts with "buy" or "hire," and if I say I have nothing, keep asking until the table has ten things on it.

---

The table is the NASA part. Nobody in Houston asked what they wished the crew had. They asked what was on board.
