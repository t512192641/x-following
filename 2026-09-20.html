The best Grok Bots I've seen so far all have basically the same shape.

One trigger. One narrow job. One thing it hands back. One human gate.

Newspaper bot: schedule fires, it gathers, PDF lands, you decide what matters. Bookmark bot: you save a link, it builds, preview shows up, you keep it or kill it.

Most people start from "build me an AI employee" and end up with something that has no clear input, no clear output, and nowhere to stop. An employee is someone you delegate to. A loop is something you can actually debug.

If you can't name all four pieces in your own Bot, you don't have a Bot yet. You have a vibe.

What does yours produce when it finishes its job?
