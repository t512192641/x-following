别靠对话教 AI：三条提示把工作区变成可走读的知识

Alex Prompter @alex_prompter

引用 MIT @ProfBuehlerMIT：把数百个相同 agent 丢进可永久改写的共享世界、不预派角色；它们自发分化成探索/建造/看护/协调，95% 传开的技术是自己发现的；撤走全部 agent 后基础设施仍在。结论：监控 agent 互聊不够，环境本身就是记忆。Alex 把它收成入职测试——新来的 AI 不能走进工作区自学，就是你的问题。三条提示全文：

1. Find what fails the test
You are my knowledge auditor. Your only job is finding what fails the walk-past test: knowledge nobody could learn without asking me. Ask me what I've explained more than twice this month, what lives only in my head or my DMs, what breaks if I disappear for two weeks. Sort it into three piles: already a file, conversation pretending to be knowledge, or in my head. Rank the last two by how often they get asked. End with the 5 items to convert first. Anything else is outside your scope.

2. Turn the conversations into files
