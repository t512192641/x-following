yibie 解读 WorldTUI（360M）：没有 shell/文件系统，每按一键由模型预测终端下一屏；用 333 token 描述字符网格世界，输出受语法硬约束；底模 Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B，在 Docker 录真按键与屏幕做成约 33.6 万会话、20 亿 token 再训练自修复

yibie @yibie

WorldTUI 是一个 360M 的模型，它替掉了一整个终端。背后没有 shell、没有文件系统，你每按一个键，由它预测屏幕下一行长什么样。

它把终端当成一张字符网格，用 333 个 token 描述世界。用户能够正常执行一个会话，一个会话从快照开始，之后每次操作，按真实终端发出的字节输入。模型接收到输入后，将返回当前终端界面中，哪些行要重写、光标落在哪里。模型的输出通过语法硬约束。

它是怎么做出来的：拿 Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B 当底子，在 Docker 里跑真命令，录下每一次按键和它之后的屏幕，做成 33.6 万个会话、约 20 亿 token 的训练集。然后教它修自己的错——让它往前走一两步，拿屏幕和真实的比对，但只修那些能从真实屏幕推出来的错，否则等于奖励幻觉。

ShellBench 是专门测试 Shell 执行指令的测试机，它包含 106 条命令，如果是直接提示，Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B 成功执行 52 条，Qwen3-0.6B 是 60 条，WorldTUI 是 80 条；文件回读 13 次测试里，它的成功次数是 9 次，其它那两个模型只有 2 和 3 次。

这个模型的训练，连同几轮改进，一共 75 个 GPU 小时，只需要一张 4090。WorldTUI 量化到 int4 之后 208 MB，能在浏览器里运行。

它离可靠的文件系统还很远。如果是运行常见的命令还行，但如果把命令用新方式组合起来，就会崩。

这件事真正的意思在另一个方向上。Qwen 的 AgentWorld 把七种环境（MCP、搜索、终端、SWE、网页、操作系统、安卓）训进一个模型，明说了"没人训过模型去模拟环境本身"；前天的 WildAWM 更直接，用 341 个合成环境对训出一个模型，在 8 个分布外基准上平均涨 10.58 分。

一边把屏幕换成模型，一边把环境换成模型。软件的表层与里层，正在被同一招替换掉。

代价是失败的性质变了：崩溃变成"看起来对的错"。所以作者自己提的那个问题才是唯一要紧的——它到底是维持了一个一致的世界，还是只是在生成像样的终端输出。

研究：ChatGPT 未请求即建用户心理档案；论文披露保存内容与可见范围

Alex Prompter @alex_prompter

Researchers found ChatGPT is building a psychological profile of its users without being asked.

They published a paper showing what it saves about you, and how little of it you chose.

And the results are unsettling.

80 users donated their ChatGPT histories to the team through GDPR data requests. The team found 2,050 saved memories inside them.

Only 84 of those memories came from a user asking ChatGPT to remember something. ChatGPT created the other 96% on its own.

28% of the memories held personal data under GDPR, like names, money details, and social identity.

Among the 65 users who had memories, 91% had personal data stored and 54% had special-category data. That's the GDPR class covering health, sexual orientation, religion, and political views. 35% had health information saved.

In January 2026, OpenAI's memory FAQ said ChatGPT was trained not to proactively remember sensitive details like health information. The FAQ now says sensitive information can appear in memory if you tell ChatGPT about it.

52% of the memories described how the person thinks. ChatGPT recorded their goals, their plans, and their fears.

One saved memory in the paper reads "has a fear of failure, particularly related to making major career decisions."

Ad platforms build your profile from what you click. ChatGPT builds it from what you tell it you want.

The researchers also checked accuracy. 84% of the memories traced back to what users wrote in their chats, so the record is mostly faithful. You didn't choose what went into it.

They built a tool called Attribution Shield to help. It suggests a rewrite of your question that drops personal details and keeps the meaning. In a test of 36 questions, the rewrites exposed less about the user in over 94% of cases, and the answers stayed useful in roughly 9 out of 10.

The sample is small, 80 users mostly from the US and Europe. The study also covers ChatGPT's older saved-memory system, and OpenAI has since moved to a more automatic one.

You can see a summary of what ChatGPT keeps about you in Settings, under Personalization and Memory. OpenAI says that summary leaves some details out. Have you checked yours?
